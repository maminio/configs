# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary user is a technical macOS power user managing many projects, workspaces,
applications, and windows through AeroSpace. They work quickly from keyboard and
pointer, rely on spatial memory, and need current context to remain obvious while
switching or reorganizing windows.

## Product Purpose

This configuration turns AeroSpace's linear workspaces into a spatial project
grid. A compact HUD provides ambient orientation, Mission Control supports direct
workspace and window management, and the app switcher provides fast scoped window
selection. Success means navigation feels immediate, predictable, and native to
macOS without requiring screenshots or invasive permissions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, native. Interfaces should feel deliberate and quietly responsive,
with enough depth and motion to communicate state without becoming decorative.

## Anti-references

- Decorative glassmorphism or blur applied everywhere.
- Glass stacked on glass, nested translucent cards, or competing material layers.
- Heavy opaque cards that obscure spatial relationships.
- Excessive accent tint where every workspace or control demands attention.
- Generic dashboard styling, dense chrome, or ornamental animation.
- Visual changes that weaken keyboard speed, direct manipulation, or privacy.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve spatial continuity. Rows, columns, focus, and drag targets must remain
   legible during every interaction.
2. Keep content primary. Navigation and controls may float above workspace content,
   but window information stays clear and visually grounded.
3. Respond immediately. Focus, hover, drag, and keyboard navigation need direct,
   low-latency feedback without distracting motion.
4. Behave like macOS. Prefer native materials, typography, interaction conventions,
   and system adaptation over hand-built visual imitation.
5. Protect privacy and focus. Use AeroSpace metadata only, avoid screenshots, and
   keep compact orientation separate from explicit window-management mode.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor macOS Reduce Transparency, Increase Contrast, and Reduce Motion settings.
Maintain readable labels, non-color-only focus indicators, sufficient separation
between layers, generous hit targets, and full keyboard operation. Effects must
degrade into clear opaque or high-contrast surfaces without losing functionality.
